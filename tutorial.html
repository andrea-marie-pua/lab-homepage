The Butterfly Effect 101: Shoelace Edition

Honestly, the butterfly bow begins similarly to a traditional shoelace knot.

The main difference is how the loops are shaped and adjusted at the end!

Follow my steps carefully until your butterfly wings begin to take shape.

Follow My Lead!

  1. Place the shoe in front of you and hold one lace in each hand.
  2. Cross the two laces and pull them tight.
  3. Fold one lace into a loop to make the first butterfly wing.
  4. Wrap the other lace around the base of the first loop.
  5. Push part of the second lace through the opening to make another loop.
  6. Pull both loops outward to form the butterfly bows.
  7. Adjust the loops until the two butterfly wings are about the same size.

A Trick for this Treat!

Keep the center knot tight while adjusting the wings! It makes a big difference in forming strong wings. Butterflies fly, not wobble!

If one wing becomes too large, gently pull the opposite loose lace.

Practice makes perfect, don't worry!
