How to Tie a Butterfly Bow on Your Shoes!

Tying your shoes with bunny ears in 2026? How dreadfully uninspired! Fear not, for this tutorial shall rescue you from the clutches of saddening mediocrity, bestow upon your footwear a touch of character, and teach you the far more elegant art of tying a butterfly-shaped bow!

What You Need

  • A pair of shoes with laces
  • Two skillful hands
  • A little inspiration

Steps

  1. Place the shoe in front of you and hold one lace in each hand.
  2. Cross the two laces and pull them tight.
  3. Fold one lace into a loop to make the first butterfly wing.
  4. Wrap the other lace around the base of the first loop.
  5. Push part of the second lace through the opening to make another loop.
  6. Pull both loops outward to form the butterfly bow.
  7. Adjust the loops until the two butterfly wings are about the same size.
